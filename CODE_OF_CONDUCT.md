# Code of Conduct

This project expects technically rigorous and respectful collaboration.

Contributors should:

- discuss evidence, code, and reproducible behavior rather than attack people;
- distinguish verified facts from assumptions and opinions;
- disclose uncertainty and conflicts in data;
- avoid fabricated adoption or authority claims;
- respect privacy, licensing, and access-control boundaries.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, deliberate misinformation, credential leakage, or attempts to pressure maintainers into misrepresenting legal-source status are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit, reject, lock, or remove contributions that violate these standards.
